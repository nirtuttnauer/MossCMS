# MossCMS logo and sign-in design

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-10-11. The user approved the logo. The working login is implemented in React/CSS; form labels and actions remain live text. The logo is also used in the sidebar and README.

## Assets

- `web/public/brand/moss-symbol-v2.png`: approved transparent logo.
- `web/public/brand/moss-arch-v2.webp`: generated login artwork, encoded as WebP for delivery.

## Final logo prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: production brand symbol for MossCMS, a self-hosted content management application, to display next to a live text wordmark.
Primary request: Create a refined new MossCMS logo symbol: a distinctive abstract lowercase m formed by two softly arching leaf-like ribbons, suggestive of moss growth and connected content. Simple memorable silhouette, intentional negative space, balanced geometry, premium editorial software brand.
Style/medium: clean flat vector-like graphic with perfectly crisp edges. Forest green #28533b, with a restrained sage green #72966b accent on one segment, no gradient.
Composition/framing: one isolated symbol centered in a square canvas, symbol fills about 82 percent of width. No surrounding badge or container.
Constraints: genuinely transparent background. Symbol only, no text or letters other than the abstract m shape. Recognizable at 24 pixels, thick balanced forms, minimal detail. No shadows, mockup, border, watermark, extra icons, or ornamental stems.
```

```text
Scene/backdrop: warm pale sage seamless studio backdrop and ground, subtle atmospheric tonal variation.
Subject: a thick softly rounded arch coated in finely detailed velvety moss, deep forest green and soft sage. Slightly asymmetric organic contours, believable fine moss fibers.
Style/medium: photorealistic art-directed botanical studio photograph, restrained museum quality.
Composition/framing: portrait 2:3 canvas, whole arch in the middle portion, generous clean upper quarter for HTML copy and open lower quarter. Frontal three-quarter view, arch about 60 percent of canvas width.
Lighting/mood: soft morning light from upper left, delicate natural grounded shadow, peaceful and refined.
Color palette: pale sage #e3e9dc backdrop, dark forest #28533b and muted sage #72966b moss.
Constraints: no text, typography, logos, people, leaves sticking out, pots, extra objects, UI, borders, watermark. This is an artwork asset only, not a screen mockup.
```
