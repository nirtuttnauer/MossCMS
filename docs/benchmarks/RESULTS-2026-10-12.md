# Capacity and recovery results

Local evidence collected 12 October 2026 for CMP-01. The baseline sustained 100k total entries for 30 minutes without request failures. Compiled-schema reuse and lookup indexes improved the matched 50k samples, but the proposed latency targets are not yet met.

This report describes migrations through 0010. The later [automatic scalar indexing report](RESULTS-AUTOMATIC-INDEXING-2026-10-12.md) records migration 0011, its 50k improvements and storage cost. Its short sample meets the 50k list target; sustained 100k validation of that build remains pending.

## Workload and source

Both runs used local Docker Desktop on macOS/ARM64, with the app and PostgreSQL each capped at 2 CPUs and 4 GiB. The load generator ran outside those caps and the host was shared with other workloads. This is not a cloud VM measurement or a competitor comparison.

The main workspace contains 80% of fixture entries and 60% of those are live: the 100k fixture exercises 80k main-workspace entries and 48k main-workspace live entries. There are three workspaces, three tiny PNG assets, and three historical revisions per entry. Text is highly compressible. Twenty delivery clients and three authenticated writers exercised lists, search, exact filters, generated localized/media/reference queries, ETags, and draft saves.

Exact snapshots, image IDs, runtimes, assertions, raw percentiles and sampled resources are in the [baseline manifest](2026-10-12-baseline/manifest.json) and [follow-up manifest](2026-10-12-optimized/manifest.json). Both images were built from copied working-tree snapshots.

## Observed latency

Nearest-rank p95 in milliseconds. Latency samples include failures; all recorded HTTP, GraphQL, transport and content error counters were zero. Smaller baseline runs and both follow-up runs were 60-second samples; the 100k baseline lasted 1,802 seconds.

| Run | Total entries | Seconds | List p95 | Generated p95 | Filter p95 | Draft save p95 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Baseline | 1,000 | 61 | 269 | 799 | 281 | 308 |
| Baseline | 10,000 | 61 | 299 | 809 | 349 | 337 |
| Baseline | 50,000 | 61 | 712 | 1111 | 2186 | 633 |
| Baseline | 100,000 | 1802 | 2200 | 3001 | 5930 | 1701 |
| Follow-up | 50,000 | 61 | 355 | 827 | 786 | 401 |
| Follow-up | 100,000 | 61 | 688 | 1548 | 2220 | 785 |

At 50k, matched 60-second samples changed list p95 from **712 ms to 355 ms**, filter p95 from **2,186 ms to 786 ms**, and draft-save p95 from **633 ms to 401 ms**. These are individual local samples, not statistically established speedups or service guarantees. The 100k follow-up is short; repeat its 30-minute run before claiming sustained performance of the new code.

The provisional 50k list target below 300 ms is still missed; the draft-save target below 750 ms is met in this sample. Larger filtered/generated reads and concurrent saves remain optimization work.

## Changes validated

- Compiled GraphQL schemas are reused in a cache of at most 128 immutable shapes. Keys include actual scoped/public field definitions and endpoint mode; resolvers use current request context. Regression tests cover workspace/token isolation, private-field changes and schema-shape changes even with equal versions.
- Migration 0010 adds partial publication-pagination and due-schedule indexes without rewriting records. Baseline plans showed full scans/sorts for delivery and full scans for the scheduler.
- Scalar filter semantics are retained. A broader JSONB index failed the existing JSON text-storage compatibility regression and was removed; future field indexing must preserve accepted values.

## Recovery and integrity

Stopped-writer backups were restored into a new empty PostgreSQL database and a new empty media volume. All recorded table and media hashes matched before app startup. Delivery, draft exclusion, nested private-field exclusion, workspace/token scope and signed original-media checks passed after recovery and restart.

| Run | Restore through verification | Backup age at final verification | Captured schedule | Later draft | Publication events |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Baseline | 37.9 seconds | 43.0 seconds | Selected revision published | Preserved | 1 after two starts |
| Follow-up | 36.2 seconds | 41.1 seconds | Selected revision published | Preserved | 1 after two starts |

Timing includes verification and repeated startup, and excludes initial backup creation. Backup-age provenance is recorded in the manifests. Fresh fixture backups with paused writers do not establish a production recovery-point or recovery-time guarantee.

The follow-up also restored revision 3 into a draft while preserving live content and its schedule, checked exact live/scheduled JSON preservation across the load, and exercised the typed delivery client over real HTTP for two pages and a scoped GraphQL failure/request ID. All checks passed.

Upgrade rehearsals preserved content/history/media from schema 0008 to 0009 in the baseline and to 0010 in the follow-up. They cover these paths only. Tiny PNGs and local storage do not establish large-media or S3 performance.
